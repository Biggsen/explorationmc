# Exploration Servers Website Planning Document

## Purpose

The Exploration Servers website serves as the public home for a collection of Minecraft servers built around exploration, discovery, travel, and adventure.

Rather than focusing on plugins or technical features, the website should showcase beautiful worlds, memorable discoveries, and the feeling of setting out into the unknown.

The website should feel closer to a travel or adventure brand than a traditional Minecraft server listing.

## A Different Way to Play

Exploration Servers are Minecraft worlds designed to reward curiosity and travel.

Venture across vast landscapes, uncover named regions and landmarks, discover hidden locations, and build your exploration record through adventure rather than grinding.

## Built for Explorers

- Discover the World. Find regions, villages, landmarks and hidden locations.
- Exploration Progression. Every discovery contributes to your explorer record.
- Unique Worlds. Each server has its own geography, lore and discoveries.
- Exploration Rewards. Unlock rewards through travel and discovery.
- Adventure and Danger. Some lands are safer than others. Preparation matters.
- Long-Term Discovery. Vast worlds designed for months of exploration.
