Exploration is Progression

Exploration is a Minecraft gamemode built around discovering the world. Travel through named regions, find villages, landmarks and hidden locations, and earn rewards as you explore. Your discoveries are tracked as you gradually uncover more of the world.

No prescribed route. No need to rush. Just a reason to keep wondering what's over the next mountain.

Built for Explorers

  • Discover

    Every region has more to uncover. Discover villages along the way, seek out its Heart somewhere on the surface, or venture underground in search of its Nerve. The journey continues in the Nether, with regions and Hearts of its own.

  • Progress

    Exploration is a path to progression. Discoveries reward armour, weapons, tools, resources, food and XP, letting you gear up simply by travelling the world. Structures also reward claim blocks for when you find somewhere worth building.

  • Face the Unknown

    Danger changes as you travel. Some regions are relatively safe, while others are home to much tougher creatures. Even seas and lakes can be dangerous, so it pays to be prepared before heading into unfamiliar lands.

  • Made for Long Journeys

    When you're always moving, the gear you carry is everything you have. If you die, you keep your inventory, so one mistake doesn't wipe out everything you've gathered along the way.
